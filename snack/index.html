A little table etiquette

This is a dessert selector inspired by backgammon, rather than a competitive match. Each point holds a recipe. Two dice move the ivory marker around the 24 points; doubles count as four moves of the same value. “Set a new table” deals 24 recipes from the full collection, bringing new treats into play.
