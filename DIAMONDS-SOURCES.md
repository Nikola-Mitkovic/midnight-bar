# Diamonds — recipe sources and choices

Researched 7 November 2026. Preparation text is expanded for home mixing. Flavor and pairing notes are editorial interpretations. Ounce displays use the rounded 30 ml bar convention.

## A ♦ Cosmopolitan

Follows IBA 40/15/15/30 ml and citrus vodka. IBA specifies lemon twist; the artwork uses orange zest as an optional house garnish.

[IBA · Cosmopolitan](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/cosmopolitan/)

## 2 ♦ Moscow Mule

Uses IBA 45/120/10 ml instead of the photographed 60/120/15 ml. Fresh serving ice is specified for the home version.

[IBA · Moscow Mule](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/moscow-mule/)

## 3 ♦ Bloody Mary

Follows IBA 45/90/15 ml and its highball-with-ice option. Gentle stirring replaces the card’s rolling method.

[IBA · Bloody Mary](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/bloody-mary/)

## 4 ♦ Espresso Martini

Uses IBA 50 ml vodka, 30 ml Kahlúa, 10 ml syrup, and one strong espresso. The source specifies a shot rather than a fixed espresso volume.

[IBA · Espresso Martini](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/espresso-martini/)

## 5 ♦ Sex on the Beach

Uses IBA’s 40/20/40/40 ml proportions and built method, replacing the card’s shaken version.

[IBA · Sex on the Beach](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/sex-on-the-beach/)

## 6 ♦ White Russian

Uses Kahlúa’s equal 30 ml vodka, coffee liqueur, and cream version, rather than the card’s larger vodka measure.

[Kahlúa · White Russian](https://www.kahlua.com/en-us/drinks/white-russian/)

## 7 ♦ Lemon Drop

Uses Diageo Bar Academy’s 1.25/0.25/0.75 oz vodka/triple-sec/lemon version, converted at 30 ml per ounce. Sugar is for the optional rim; no syrup is mixed in. The illustration omits the source’s lemon wheel, which is optional here.

[Diageo Bar Academy · Lemon Drop Martini](https://www.diageobaracademy.com/en-us/home/explore-all-recipes/smirnoff-lemon-drop-martini)

## 8 ♦ Tequila Sunrise

Uses IBA 45/90/15 ml rather than the larger photographed pour. Orange follows IBA; the illustrated cherry is an optional house addition.

[IBA · Tequila Sunrise](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/tequila-sunrise/)

## 9 ♦ Paloma

Follows IBA 50/5/100 ml with a pinch of salt in the glass. This replaces the card’s salt rim and larger lime measure.

[IBA · Paloma](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/paloma/)

## 10 ♦ Tommy’s Margarita

Uses the current IBA 60/30/30 ml formula, including 30 ml agave nectar rather than the photographed 15 ml. Served over ice with lime.

[IBA · Tommy’s Margarita](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/tommys-margarita/)

## J ♦ El Diablo

Follows Patrón’s official recipe video: 45/30/30/7.5 ml and reposado tequila. Its explicit method resolves the website’s ambiguous instruction about when to add cassis.

[Patrón · El Diablo official recipe video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5MZLcByZJPE)

## Q ♦ Margarita

Uses IBA 50/20/15 ml and an optional half-salt rim, replacing the card’s 60/30/30 ml formula. Served up without a lime-wheel garnish.

[IBA · Margarita](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/margarita/)

## K ♦ Batanga

Follows El Tequileño’s 2 oz tequila, whole lime, and cola-to-top recipe. A barspoon replaces the traditional stirring knife for this home version.

[El Tequileño · La Batanga](https://www.tequileno.com/la-batanga-cocktail)
