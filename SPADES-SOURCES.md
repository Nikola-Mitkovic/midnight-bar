# Spades — recipe sources and choices

Researched 6 November 2026. Preparation text is expanded for home mixing. Flavor and pairing notes are editorial suggestions inferred from ingredients, not claims from the recipe sources. Ounce displays use the rounded 30 ml bar convention.

## A ♠ Old Fashioned

IBA’s 45 ml whiskey replaces the photographed 60 ml. Orange zest and cherry follow its garnish options.

[IBA · Old Fashioned](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/old-fashioned/)

## 2 ♠ Manhattan

Uses IBA 50/20 ml measures and one dash of bitters instead of the card’s 60/30 ml and two dashes.

[IBA · Manhattan](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/manhattan/)

## 3 ♠ Whiskey Sour

Uses IBA 45/25/20 ml measures; optional egg white is specified as pasteurized for home mixing.

[IBA · Whiskey Sour](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/whiskey-sour/)

## 4 ♠ Mint Julep

Uses IBA 60 ml bourbon, mint sprigs, powdered sugar, and water in place of the card’s syrup formula.

[IBA · Mint Julep](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/mint-julep/)

## 5 ♠ Sazerac

Follows Sazerac House’s rye-and-Herbsaint version, preserving the whiskey suit. IBA instead specifies cognac and absinthe. The card’s syrup is replaced by a sugar cube.

[Sazerac House · Official recipe (PDF)](https://www.sazerachouse.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Sazerac_Pamphlet_SazHouse_RGB-Pages.pdf)

## 6 ♠ Boulevardier

Uses IBA 45/30/30 ml and an up serve; the photographed version uses 37.5 ml bourbon and a rocks glass.

[IBA · Boulevardier](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/boulevardier/)

## 7 ♠ Irish Coffee

Uses IBA 50 ml whiskey and 50 ml cream; retains 120 ml coffee. No garnish is specified.

[IBA · Irish Coffee](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/irish-coffee/)

## 8 ♠ Rusty Nail

Uses IBA 45 ml Scotch and 25 ml Drambuie, slightly more liqueur than the photographed 22.5 ml.

[IBA · Rusty Nail](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/rusty-nail/)

## 9 ♠ Penicillin

Uses IBA’s fresh ginger plus honey syrup instead of the card’s combined honey-ginger syrup. Lagavulin 16 is the named IBA float; other smoky Islay Scotch is an explicit substitution.

[IBA · Penicillin](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/penicillin/)

## 10 ♠ Rob Roy

Retains the photographed 60/30 ml proportions, matching Chivas’s 2:1 recipe with two dashes of bitters. A coupe replaces its martini glass.

[Chivas Regal · Rob Roy](https://www.chivas.com/en-us/cocktails/rob-roy/)

## J ♠ New York Sour

Uses IBA 60/30/22.5/15 ml measures. Egg white is made optional and pasteurized; the wine is reserved for the float, never shaken with the base.

[IBA · New York Sour](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/new-york-sour/)

## Q ♠ Godfather

Uses The Glenlivet’s 45/15 ml formula rather than the card’s 45/22.5 ml. Its orange-and-cherry garnish is optional here.

[The Glenlivet · The Godfather](https://www.theglenlivet.com/en/whisky-cocktails/the-godfather/)

## K ♠ Blood and Sand

Retains the photographed equal-parts classic described in Difford’s review, not the tweaked recipe at the top of that page. Orange zest is our house garnish.

[Difford’s Guide · Classic formula discussion](https://www.diffordsguide.com/cocktails/recipe/556/blood-and-sand-diffords-recipe)
