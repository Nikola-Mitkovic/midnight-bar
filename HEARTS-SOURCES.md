# Hearts edition — recipe choices

Researched 6 November 2026. The hearts recipes now follow the sources linked below; differences from the photographed cards are disclosed on each card back. Preparation explanations and bartender notes are original editorial writing. Artwork is illustrative; optional garnish differences are noted. Measurements use 30 ml per recipe ounce for the rounded oz display.

## A ♥ Daiquiri

IBA measures replace the photographed syrup version. The lime-wheel garnish is optional; IBA specifies none.

[IBA · Daiquiri](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/daiquiri/)

## 2 ♥ Cuba Libre

Uses IBA measures: 50 ml rum, 120 ml cola, and 10 ml lime; slightly different from the photographed card.

[IBA · Cuba Libre](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/cuba-libre/)

## 3 ♥ Mai Tai

The IBA two-rum formula replaces the card’s single aged rum and adds simple syrup. A lime shell in the artwork is decorative.

[IBA · Mai-Tai](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/mai-tai/)

## 4 ♥ Piña Colada

Uses IBA 50/30/50 ml proportions instead of the photographed 60/45/60 ml recipe.

[IBA · Piña Colada](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/pina-colada/)

## 5 ♥ Dark ’n’ Stormy

IBA version: Goslings rum, ginger beer, and lime garnish. The photographed added lime juice is omitted.

[IBA · Dark ’N’ Stormy](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/dark-n-stormy/)

## 6 ♥ Painkiller

Pusser’s 2:4:1:1 formula fills the former 6♥ Moscow Mule slot. The 2♦ vodka Mule remains.

[Pusser’s · Painkiller](https://pussersrum.com/blogs/logbook/best-painkiller-cocktail-recipes-for-a-soothing-night-in)

## 7 ♥ Hemingway Daiquiri

Keeps the familiar menu name but uses IBA Hemingway Special measures. IBA specifies no garnish.

[IBA · Hemingway Special](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/hemingway-special/)

## 8 ♥ Planter’s Punch

This is the current IBA cane-juice version, not the photographed syrup-and-bitters variation.

[IBA · Planters Punch](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/planters-punch/)

## 9 ♥ Hurricane

Uses BACARDÍ’s US recipe, with less rum than the photographed card. Other Hurricane versions differ.

[BACARDÍ US · Hurricane](https://www.bacardi.com/us/en/rum-cocktails/hurricane/)

## 10 ♥ Caipirinha

IBA supplies the card’s missing spirit amount and specifies four teaspoons of sugar.

[IBA · Caipirinha](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/caipirinha/)

## J ♥ Jack Rose

Measures match Laird’s published recipe and the photographed card. Expanded steps and garnish are house guidance.

[Laird & Company · Jack Rose](https://www.lairdandcompany.com/all-cocktails/jack-rose-cocktail)

## Q ♥ Ti’ Punch

Uses Clément’s 50 ml rum and teaspoon of sugar/syrup, rather than the photographed 60/15 ml formula.

[Rhum Clément · Ti’Punch](https://www.rhum-clement.com/en/cocktail/tipunch/)

## K ♥ Mojito

Follows IBA rum, lime, and sugar measures. IBA lists six mint sprigs; use small sprigs that fit the glass.

[IBA · Mojito](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/mojito/)
