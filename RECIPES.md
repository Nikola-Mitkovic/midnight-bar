# The Midnight Bar — Full deck

54 photographed cards; 53 distinct cocktail names. Moscow Mule appears twice with different garnishes. Measures use 30 ml per recipe ounce. Preparation wording is expanded for usability. Unspecified quantities and the added cachaça measure are explained in the notes.

## A ♠ Old Fashioned

- 60 ml (2 oz) bourbon whiskey
- 1 (1) sugar cube
- 2 dashes (2 dashes) Angostura bitters
- 1 (1) orange peel

1. Muddle the sugar with the bitters and a small splash of water until dissolved.
2. Add bourbon and ice, then stir until chilled. Express the orange peel over the glass and add it.

Note: Use fresh ice and chill the glass before serving.
