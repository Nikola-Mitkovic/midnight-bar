# The Midnight Bar — 54 distinct recipes

All four standard suits have researched backs. See their source guides. The two jokers retain their card transcriptions. Flavor and pairing notes are editorial suggestions.

## A ♠ Old Fashioned

- 45 ml (1.5 oz) bourbon or rye whiskey
- 1 (1) sugar cube
- A few dashes (A few dashes) Angostura bitters
- A few dashes (A few dashes) water
- To garnish (To garnish) orange zest and cocktail cherry

Dissolve sugar with bitters and water. Add ice and whiskey; stir and garnish.

One thing to learn: Expressing citrus means bending the peel, coloured side toward the drink, to spray aromatic oils onto its surface.

What to expect: A slow, whiskey-led sip: oak, caramel, orange oil, and baking spice, with sweetness rounding the edges. Try alongside toasted pecans.

## 2 ♠ Manhattan

- 50 ml (1.67 oz) rye whiskey
- 20 ml (0.67 oz) sweet red vermouth
- 1 dash (1 dash) Angostura bitters
- 1 (1) cocktail cherry

Stir whiskey, vermouth, and bitters with ice; strain into a chilled coupe and add a cherry.

One thing to learn: Stirring chills and dilutes without the aeration of shaking, keeping this spirit-led drink clear and silky.

What to expect: Rye spice meets dark fruit and herbal sweetness, with a gently bitter finish. A small piece of aged cheddar makes a savory contrast.

## 3 ♠ Whiskey Sour

- 45 ml (1.5 oz) bourbon whiskey
- 25 ml (0.83 oz) fresh lemon juice
- 20 ml (0.67 oz) simple syrup
- A few drops, optional (A few drops, optional) pasteurized egg white
- To garnish (To garnish) half orange slice and cocktail cherry

Shake bourbon, lemon, syrup, and optional egg white with ice. Strain over fresh ice; garnish.

One thing to learn: Egg white adds foam and a softer texture rather than sweetness. The drink also works without it.

What to expect: Bright lemon first, then rounded bourbon and a sweet-tart finish. With egg white it feels softer and silkier; without, more direct and crisp.

## 4 ♠ Mint Julep

## 7 ♠ Irish Coffee

What to expect: Hot, lightly sweet coffee under cool, rich cream, with mellow whiskey warmth. A little shortbread suits its coffee-and-cream character.

- 22.5 ml (0.75 oz) BACARDÍ Superior rum
- 30 ml (1 oz) orange juice
- 30 ml (1 oz) fresh lime juice
- 30 ml (1 oz) passion-fruit syrup
- 1 tsp (1 tsp) grenadine
- To garnish (To garnish) lemon wheel, orange peel, and cherry

Shake both rums, juices, and syrups with ice. Strain over fresh ice in a hurricane glass.

One thing to learn: Passion-fruit syrup is sweetened. Juice or purée is not a direct one-for-one substitute.

What to expect: Passion fruit and orange with tart lime and a little grenadine sweetness. Juicy, bright, and tropical.

## 10 ♥ Caipirinha

- 60 ml (2 oz) cachaça
- 1 (1) lime, cut into small wedges
- 4 tsp (4 tsp) white cane sugar
- To fill (To fill) cracked ice

Gently muddle lime with sugar. Fill with cracked ice, add cachaça, and stir.

One thing to learn: Cachaça is the defining spirit here. Muddling releases juice and peel oils; a gentle touch limits bitterness.

What to expect: Zesty lime oils, fresh lime juice, and a grassy cane-spirit character. Sweet-tart with a lively, rustic texture.

## J ♥ Jack Rose

- 60 ml (2 oz) Laird’s Applejack
- 22.5 ml (0.75 oz) fresh lemon juice
- 15 ml (0.5 oz) pomegranate grenadine
- Optional (Optional) lemon twist

Shake applejack, lemon, and grenadine with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe.

One thing to learn: Grenadine is pomegranate-based syrup. Applejack gives this sour its apple character; it is not a rum drink.

What to expect: Apple-brandy warmth, lemon brightness, and a pomegranate finish. Fruity but still a compact, tart cocktail.

## Q ♥ Ti’ Punch

- 50 ml (1.67 oz) white rhum agricole
- 1 (1) lime wedge or cheek
- 1 tsp (1 tsp) cane sugar or cane syrup

Press lime into sugar, stir, then add rhum agricole and stir again. Serve in a small glass.

One thing to learn: Rhum agricole is distilled from fresh sugar-cane juice rather than molasses. Check the bottle label: ordinary white rum is not the same ingredient.

What to expect: A short, concentrated sip with grassy cane character, a little sweetness, and lime-peel aroma. No juice-heavy tropical softness.

## K ♥ Mojito

- 45 ml (1.5 oz) white Cuban rum
- 20 ml (0.67 oz) fresh lime juice
- 6 small sprigs (6 small sprigs) mint
- 2 tsp (2 tsp) white cane sugar
- To top (To top) soda water
- To garnish (To garnish) mint sprig and lime slice

Gently press mint with sugar and lime. Add ice and rum, top with soda, and stir lightly.

One thing to learn: The aroma of intact mint is part of the drink. Gentle mixing preserves both fresh fragrance and soda bubbles.

What to expect: Fresh mint, bright lime, and light sweetness with crisp soda bubbles. A refreshing partner for grilled vegetables or seafood.

## JOKER ★ Zombie

- 45 ml (1.5 oz) white rum
- 45 ml (1.5 oz) gold rum
- 30 ml (1 oz) dark rum (151 proof)
- 30 ml (1 oz) lime juice
- 15 ml (0.5 oz) pineapple juice
- 15 ml (0.5 oz) passion fruit syrup
- 1 tsp (1 tsp) brown sugar
- 1 dash (1 dash) bitters

Shake all ingredients with ice until the sugar is dissolved. Pour into a tiki mug over fresh ice and garnish with tropical fruit.
