Hearts have researched recipe backs; other suits retain the photographed-card recipes. See HEARTS-SOURCES.md for recipe choices.

## A ♠ Old Fashioned

- 60 ml (2 oz) bourbon whiskey
- 1 (1) sugar cube
- 2 dashes (2 dashes) Angostura bitters
- 1 (1) orange peel

Muddle the sugar with the bitters and a small splash of water until dissolved. Add bourbon and ice, then stir until chilled. Express the orange peel over the glass and add it.

Bartender’s note: Use fresh ice and chill the glass before serving.

## 2 ♠ Manhattan

- 60 ml (2 oz) rye whiskey
- 30 ml (1 oz) sweet vermouth
- 2 dashes (2 dashes) Angostura bitters
- 1 (1) cocktail cherry

Stir the rye, vermouth, and bitters with ice until chilled. Strain into a chilled coupe and garnish with the cherry.

Bartender’s note: Refrigerate opened vermouth to keep its aromas fresh.
