# Café menu presentation

Recipes and quantities live in `recipes.js`; detailed preparation and flavor notes live in `details.js`. `menu-metadata.js` defines coffee-base tags and French wheel-sector labels. The preview description reuses each drink's existing flavor note.

Coffee-base tags describe the recipe input (grounds, prepared espresso, brewed coffee, or instant coffee). They do not imply different bean varieties. Filter by style and base, search text, or tap a card tag; grid and swipe modes share these filters. Clear filters appears only with an active filter or search. Roulette ingredient exclusions remain independent of menu browsing.

Sector labels follow the single-zero European wheel: 17 Voisins du Zéro, 12 Tiers du Cylindre, and 8 Orphelins. Reference: https://www.playsmart.ca/table-games/roulette/how-to-play/european-roulette/ . These are decorative grouping labels in this coffee picker.

Swipe mode supports horizontal dragging, Previous/Next buttons, and left/right arrow keys. Reduced-motion preferences disable card motion and button transitions. Ingredients and amounts remain in the recipe dialog rather than the preview card.
