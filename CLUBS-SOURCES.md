# Clubs — recipe sources and choices

Researched 6 November 2026. Preparation text is expanded for home mixing. Flavor and pairing notes are editorial interpretations. Ounce displays use the rounded 30 ml bar convention.

## A ♣ Dry Martini

IBA 60/10 ml replaces the photographed 75/15 ml. The art shows both garnish choices; use either.

[IBA · Dry Martini](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/dry-martini/)

## 2 ♣ Gin & Tonic

Uses Bombay Sapphire’s 50/100 ml ratio. A highball replaces the source’s balloon glass to match the deck.

[Bombay Sapphire · Gin & Tonic](https://www.bombaysapphire.com/gin-cocktails/bombay-sapphire-and-tonic/23153/)

## 3 ♣ Gimlet

Uses Plymouth Gin’s fresh-lime 50/25/15 ml formula, served up to retain the card’s coupe rather than over ice as in the source.

[Plymouth Gin · Gimlet](https://www.plymouthgin.com/en/cocktail/gimlet/)

## 4 ♣ Tom Collins

Follows Hayman’s Old Tom 50/25/15 ml recipe with rich syrup. The illustrated lemon wheel replaces its lemon peel; soda is never shaken.

[Hayman’s Gin · Tom Collins](https://www.haymansgin.com/cocktails/tomcollins/)

## 5 ♣ Aviation

Uses IBA 45/15/15 ml plus one barspoon violette, rather than the stronger photographed proportions.

[IBA · Aviation](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/aviation/)

## 6 ♣ Bee’s Knees

Follows Barr Hill’s lemon-and-honey version, matching the deck’s character. Its 2:1 honey syrup is specified; IBA lists a different version containing orange juice.

[Barr Hill · Bee’s Knees](https://www.barrhill.com/pages/bees-knees-cocktail)

## 7 ♣ The Last Word

Matches IBA’s four equal 22.5 ml measures and no-garnish serve.

[IBA · The Last Word](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/last-word/)

## 8 ♣ Singapore Sling

Uses the full IBA formula, adding Bénédictine, grenadine, bitters, and a measured pineapple portion to the simplified card. Fresh serving ice is our house presentation.

[IBA · Singapore Sling](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/singapore-sling/)

## 9 ♣ Vesper

Uses IBA 45/15/7.5 ml, half the photographed card’s measures but the same proportions. Shaking follows this recipe’s specified method.

[IBA · Vesper](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/vesper/)

## 10 ♣ White Lady

Uses IBA’s 40/30/20 ml egg-free formula, replacing the photographed proportions and optional egg white.

[IBA · White Lady](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/white-lady/)

## J ♣ Negroni

Matches IBA’s equal 30 ml measures. Half an orange slice replaces the photographed orange peel.

[IBA · Negroni](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/negroni/)

## Q ♣ French 75

Uses IBA’s 60 ml Champagne rather than the card’s 90 ml; other measures match. No garnish is specified.

[IBA · French 75](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/french-75/)

## K ♣ Corpse Reviver #2

Uses IBA’s four equal 30 ml measures and an absinthe dash, instead of the card’s 22.5 ml measures and glass rinse. The name makes no health claim.

[IBA · Corpse Reviver #2](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/corpse-reviver-2/)
